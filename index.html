Why Do We Serve ONLY 3 Items?

Godaak kade walata gihilla menu eke items 50k balala confuse wenna epa machn! We spent hundreds of nights refining three cheese kottu recipes to absolute culinary gold.

Double Blend Cheese

Imported high-grade Mozzarella for that legendary long pull, combined with creamy dairy cheddar sauce melted straight into the godamba shreds.

Slow-Simmered Gravy

Our secret weapon: a 6-hour spiced curry kettle with roasted Lankan spices, curry leaves, and tempered shallots poured sizzling hot on the plate.

Fresh Kadai Action

Never pre-mixed or microwaved. Made directly under the rhythmic beat of cast iron steel blades the very second you tap order.
